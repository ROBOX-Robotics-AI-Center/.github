ROBOX Robotics & AI Center is an Ethiopian robotics engineering and technology innovation center focused on building practical solutions, developing technical skills, and connecting education with real-world engineering.

We work across robotics, artificial intelligence, automation, digital manufacturing, 3D printing, CNC technologies, embedded systems, and engineering design.

Our goal is simple: **turn ideas into working solutions and help build the next generation of engineers and innovators.**

---

## What We Do

ROBOX brings together engineering, technology, education, and practical innovation through:

* 🤖 Robotics and intelligent systems
* 🧠 Artificial Intelligence
* ⚙️ Industrial automation and control
* 🖨️ 3D printing and rapid prototyping
* 🏭 CNC and digital manufacturing
* 🔧 Engineering design and product development
* 🔌 Arduino, embedded systems, and electronics
* 🎓 Technical training and engineering education
* 🚀 Prototyping, innovation, and applied research

---

## Engineering & Innovation

We believe engineering is best learned and developed by building real things.

Our work combines mechanical engineering, electronics, software, automation, and digital manufacturing to develop practical prototypes and technology solutions.

From concept and design to prototyping and implementation, we focus on solving real problems through engineering.

---

## Education & Training

ROBOX is committed to helping students, engineers, and aspiring innovators develop practical technical skills.

Our programs provide hands-on exposure to areas including:

* Robotics
* Artificial Intelligence
* 3D modeling and printing
* CNC and manufacturing
* Automation
* Arduino and electronics
* Engineering design
* Programming and technology

We also support internships, externships, mentorship, competitions, and collaborative projects that help bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practice.

---

## Industry & Collaboration

We work to connect Ethiopian engineers, students, universities, manufacturers, startups, and technology organizations with modern engineering technologies and practical expertise.

Our collaborations are focused on:

* Engineering and manufacturing
* Digital transformation
* Technology education
* Research and innovation
* Prototyping and product development
* Industry-academia collaboration

ROBOX is also an official SOLIDWORKS Authorized Sub-Reseller in Ethiopia, providing SOLIDWORKS licensing, training, local technical support, and engineering consulting services.
